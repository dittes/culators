How density is calculated

Calculate density as mass divided by volume and show the equivalent in common SI units. Mass and volume must describe the same sample at the same conditions; porous materials, mixtures, and temperature-sensitive fluids may need a more specific measurement method. A 500 g object occupying 200 cm³ has density 2.5 g/cm³. The calculation validates each input and keeps its stated units explicit. Density is mass per volume; weight is gravitational force. This is an estimate for the stated assumptions; confirm measurements and any applicable professional or local standards before relying on it.

density = mass ÷ volume

Frequently asked questions

How is density different from weight?

Density is mass per volume; weight is gravitational force.

Can volume be zero?

No. A positive volume is required to define density.
