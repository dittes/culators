How to use this calculator

  1. Enter weight lifted, repetitions completed, estimate formula.
  2. Select Calculate to see the answer and its breakdown.
  3. Keep a scenario, change an input and compare the results.

Use consistent units and check the assumptions below before applying the result.

How one rep max is calculated

Epley: 1RM = load×(1+reps/30). Brzycki: 1RM = load×36/(37−reps). Both are estimates and become less dependable at high repetition counts. A 100 kg set of 5 reps estimates about 116.7 kg by Epley and 112.5 kg by Brzycki.

Assumptions & useful context

Prediction only; it is not a tested maximum or a safety recommendation.

Health calculations are estimates for education. They cannot diagnose a condition or replace a clinician’s assessment. Individual needs vary, especially during pregnancy, illness and growth.

Frequently asked questions

Should I attempt the estimated maximum?

Not based on this number alone; use safe technique, suitable supervision, and your training context.

Why do formulas disagree?

They fit different observed lifting data and approximate the strength-repetition relationship differently.
